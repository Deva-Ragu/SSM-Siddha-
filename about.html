Our Story

Sri Sanjeevi Siddha Marundhagam

Founded with a deep respect for traditional Siddha medicine, Sri Sanjeevi Siddha Marundhagam is dedicated to bringing the healing power of ancient herbal wisdom to people seeking natural, effective solutions for their hair, skin, and overall wellness.

Our journey began with a simple belief: that nature provides everything we need for health and beauty. Drawing from centuries-old Siddha formulations, we create products that are 100% herbal, ethically sourced, and crafted with care.

Every product we make—from our bestselling Uvathi Hair Oil to our healing ointments and wellness remedies—reflects our commitment to quality, authenticity, and the timeless wisdom of Siddha medicine.
